? Quick guide — how it works
  1. Interfaces — put your Wi-Fi adapter into monitor mode (needed to scan & capture).
  2. Scan — pick the monitor interface and a band (2.4 / 5 GHz), Start Scan, then click a network row to load it as the target. Green badges = crackable WPA2.
  3. Capture — on the target, hit Capture Handshake; optionally Deauth a client to force a reconnect. The live panel shows data packets, clients, and a 🎉 when a handshake lands.
  4. Wordgen — build a wordlist (target words + mutations, or phone/charset patterns). It streams straight into your wordlists folder.
  5. Crack — pick the captured .cap + a wordlist → Analyze. The dashboard shows speed, the passphrase being tried, and the key if found.
  6. Files / Handshakes — manage captures (auto-flagged for handshakes) and wordlists (with line counts & preview).

Tip: 5 GHz networks need a 5 GHz-capable adapter, and deauth is often blocked there — target the 2.4 GHz twin of the same router (same password).
